functions .....

functions have a name and any number of arguments including zero. arguments can have defaults.

functions can "do things" and/or return things.

functions can be named and used at will ...

or you can just plop them wherever, in the argument of another function, or just out on its own:

(function(){
    return "shoe,shoe,shoe,shoe,shoe";
})().split(",");

functions also obey scope, they can be part of other functions ... or they can be "methods" of objects.
